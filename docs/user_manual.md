# Farmer User Manual — KrishiMitra (शेतकरी मार्गदर्शिका / किसान मार्गदर्शिका)

## How to Use KrishiMitra

### 1. Choose Your Language (भाषा निवडा / भाषा चुनें)
Click **मराठी**, **हिन्दी**, or **English** in the left menu.

### 2. Ask by Voice (माइकवर बोला / माइक पर बोलें)
1. Click the **Speak on Mic (माइकवर बोला)** button.
2. Ask your question clearly (for example: *"कापसाच्या पानांवर पिवळे डाग पडत आहेत, काय करावे?"*).
3. Click **Stop & Submit**. You will see your recognized speech, written answer, source references, and an **Audio Player** to hear the answer!

### 3. Ask by Text or Quick Examples
Type your question in the text box or tap any **Quick Example** button.

### 4. Check Live Weather & Mandi Prices
- Open **Weather & Forecast** to see today's temperature, rain chance, 3-day outlook, and farming advice for your district.
- Open **Mandi Prices** to check APMC commodity rates.

### 5. Safety Reminder
Never spray unverified pesticides. For emergency crop advice, call the free **National Kisan Call Centre: `1800-180-1551`**.
